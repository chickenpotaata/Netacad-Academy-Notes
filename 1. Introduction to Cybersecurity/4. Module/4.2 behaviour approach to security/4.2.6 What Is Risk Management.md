
Risk management is the formal process of continuously identifying and assessing risk in an effort to reduce the impact of threats and vulnerabilities.

identify the threats that increase the risks
- processes, products, attack vectors

assess the risks
- some threats may bring the whole company on standstill, some may be minor inconvenciencies
- categorized by financial impact and technical complexity

respond to the risk
- develop a plan to reduce the risk to the organization overall
- detailed analysis where the risk can be minimized, mitigated, or just accepted

monitor the risk
- continously update the risks, eliminate the previous ones and keep tabs on each of the prioritites
- you need to keep monitoring on threats that exists but cannot be completely taken off
