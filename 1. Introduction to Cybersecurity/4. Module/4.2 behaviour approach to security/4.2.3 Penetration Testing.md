penetration test can be divided into 5 different step

planning
- either by actively or passively scanning the attacker can discover vulnerabilities misconfigurations, weak entry points, etc.
scanning
- port scanning to identify potential access points into a target system
- vulnerability scanning to identify potential exploitable vulnerabilities of a particular target
gaining access
- in this step the attacker gains the foothold onto the systems and starts doing their stuff
- aunching an exploit with a payload onto the system
- breaching physical barriers to assets
maintaining access
- The pen tester will maintain access to the target to find out what data and systems are vulnerable to exploitation.
- in this step its crucial to stay undetected
- deploying backdoors and malware(if one is blakc) is common in this step
analysis and reporting
- The pen tester will provide feedback via a report that recommends updates to products, policies and training to improve an organization’s security.
