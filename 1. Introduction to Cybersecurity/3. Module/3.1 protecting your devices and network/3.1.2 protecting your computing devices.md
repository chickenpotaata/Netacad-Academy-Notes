Your computing devices are the portal to your online life, storing a lot of your personal data.
- so secure them properly

You should use at least one type of firewall (either a software firewall or a hardware firewall on a router) to protect your device from unauthorized access.

preferrably you should also have the most basic antivirus installed. Though for example on macos its thought that they wont need such things, they are wrong.

Update patches on your software and operating system regurarly

also preferrably setup proper password protection
- use different password on different services
