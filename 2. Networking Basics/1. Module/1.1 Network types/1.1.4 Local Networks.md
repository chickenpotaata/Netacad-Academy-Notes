local networks can be divided roughly into 4 different types

small home networks
- it just connects couple of computers to the internet

small office and home office networks (soho)
- these networks are configured so that they can connect the devices inside the network to a corporate network that is centralized, shared resource

Medium to large networks
- Medium to large networks, such as those used by corporations and schools, can have many locations with hundreds or thousands of interconnected hosts.

World wide networks
- The internet is a network of networks that connects hundreds of millions of computers world-wide.
